ABOUT THE HACK

One world. Every builder starts on flat ground.

OVERHACK is a 24-hour hackathon for students who'd rather build than pitch slideware. You get a blank world, a stack of tools, and one night to turn a raw idea into something that runs. No pre-built kits, no shortcuts — just you, your team, and the crafting table.

  • ✓ Open to all skill levels — solo or squads of up to 4
  • ✓ Mentors roam the map all night to help you respawn from bugs
  • ✓ Build on any stack — web, mobile, hardware, or game
  • ✓ Judged on craft, not just concept
➜

idea + code + coffee + time = shipped

SURVIVAL GUIDE

Rules & eligibility

Who can join

Open to all current undergrad and postgrad students. Teams of 1–4. Bring a valid student ID.

Original builds only

Start from scratch at kickoff. Pre-built projects, templates as your whole submission, or resubmitted past projects are disqualified.

Open toolset

Any language, framework, or hardware is fair game. Public libraries, APIs, and AI coding tools are allowed — be ready to explain what you used and why.

Submission

Public GitHub repo with a clear README, plus a 3-minute demo at closing ceremony. Late submissions are not judged.
